StarPath brings glanceable Google Maps navigation directions directly to your Amazfit Active 2 smartwatch while your phone stays safely in your pocket or bag.

★ How It Works
StarPath reads Google Maps navigation notifications, reformats them into high-contrast directional cards, and mirrors them over Bluetooth Low Energy via the Zepp app to your watch.

★ Key Features
• Large Directional Badges: Custom rendered high-contrast turn arrows designed specifically for quick glances on round smartwatch displays.
• Smart Milestone Alerts: Automatically alerts and wakes the watch display at key approach milestones (500m, 200m, 100m, 50m).
• Stay-Awake Pulses: Sends gentle keep-alive pulses approaching intersections so turn instructions remain visible on screen.
• Automatic Lifecycle: Background service activates when Google Maps navigation starts and cleanly shuts down when you reach your destination.
• Battery Efficient: Suppresses alerts during long straight cruising to minimize watch and phone battery drain.
• 100% Privacy & Zero Telemetry: Completely offline. No internet permission, no analytics, no ads, and zero data collection.

★ Supported Languages
Maneuver and distance parsing currently supports English and Vietnamese, with additional language contributions welcome.
